South Surrey Homes for Sale – Estates, Townhomes & Beachside Properties

South Surrey is one of Surrey's six town centres, on the Semiahmoo Peninsula between the city's northern communities and the U.S. border at the Peace Arch crossing. It's a market built around larger lots, golf courses, and beach access, with a lower‑density feel than many parts of Surrey. With hundreds of active listings, prices range widely, from a townhome to a golf‑course or waterfront home. Whether you're after a golf‑course home in Morgan Creek, a cottage in Crescent Beach, or a family home in Grandview Heights, South Surrey trades density for space.

Is South Surrey a good place to invest in real estate right now?

South Surrey offers a mix of townhomes, condos and single‑family homes, with larger lots in many pockets and Highway 99 access to the border and Vancouver. Talk to Nader for a current‑day read on a specific street or community.

What's the property transfer tax on a home in South Surrey, BC?

BC's property transfer tax is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% from $2,000,000 to $3,000,000, and a further 2% (5% total) on any residential value above $3,000,000. South Surrey's range spans from townhomes and condos, some near the first‑time buyer exemption threshold (up to an $835,000 fair market value), to larger homes that sit above it. Use the Cash to Close calculator for an exact number.

How far is South Surrey from downtown Vancouver?

By car it's roughly a 40 to 60 minute drive via Highway 99, depending on the neighbourhood and traffic. There's no SkyTrain station in South Surrey itself, so check TransLink for current transit options.

Is South Surrey a separate city from Surrey?

No. South Surrey is one of Surrey's six town centres, not a separate municipality, but it's searched separately because its housing stock and price points differ from much of the rest of the city.

What school district serves South Surrey?

Public schools fall under School District 36 Surrey, the same district that covers the rest of Surrey.
