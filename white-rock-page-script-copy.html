White Rock page: Page Script to paste

Step 1. Click Copy all. Step 2. In Lofty open Edit Page for White Rock Listings and scroll down to the Page Script box. Step 3. Click in the box, press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+V (this replaces the console.log line). Step 4. Click Save, then Publish. If the button does nothing, click inside the box below, press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C.
