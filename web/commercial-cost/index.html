Estimate only, for BC commercial property. Property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% up to $2,000,000 and 3% above. The extra 2% over $3,000,000, the first-time buyer and newly built home exemptions, the foreign buyer tax and the speculation and vacancy tax apply to residential property only. GST: most commercial sales are taxable at 5%. A GST-registered buyer self-assesses it and normally claims it back as an input tax credit. Mixed-use and multi-family properties are taxed differently on their residential part. Legal, appraisal, environmental and building assessment costs are typical estimates. Excludes lender fees, property tax and rent adjustments. Confirm with your lawyer and accountant.
