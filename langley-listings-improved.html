Frequently Asked Questions About Langley Real Estate

Is Langley a good place to invest in real estate right now?

Langley is in the middle of two long‑term changes at once: the Surrey‑Langley SkyTrain extension under construction, and the ongoing build‑out of Willoughby. Both tend to support demand along their corridors, though timing and location within Langley matter a lot. Talk to Nader for a read on a specific area.

What's the property transfer tax on a home in Langley, BC?

BC's property transfer tax is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% from $2,000,000 to $3,000,000, and a further 2% (5% total) on any residential value above $3,000,000. Some Willoughby condos and townhomes can qualify for the first‑time buyer exemption (up to an $835,000 fair market value), while larger acreages can land in the higher tax tiers. Use the Cash to Close calculator for an exact number.

How far is Langley from downtown Vancouver?

About 45 to 60 minutes by car via Highway 1, depending on traffic and which part of Langley you're in. Transit times are expected to improve once the Surrey‑Langley SkyTrain extension opens, which is currently scheduled for late 2029.

Is Langley part of Metro Vancouver?

Yes. Both the Township of Langley and the City of Langley are member municipalities of the Metro Vancouver Regional District.

What's the difference between the Township of Langley and the City of Langley?

They're two separate municipalities with their own councils, even though they share a name and are usually searched and marketed together. The City of Langley is the smaller, denser core; the Township surrounds it and includes Willoughby, Walnut Grove, Fort Langley, Brookswood, Murrayville, and Aldergrove.
