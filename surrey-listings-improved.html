Frequently Asked Questions About Surrey Real Estate

Is Surrey a good place to invest in real estate right now?

Surrey's population growth and the Surrey‑Langley SkyTrain extension under construction are two factors many buyers watch, particularly along Fraser Highway. Talk to Nader for a current‑day read on a specific town centre.

What's the property transfer tax on a home in Surrey, BC?

BC's property transfer tax is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% from $2,000,000 to $3,000,000, and a further 2% (5% total) on any residential value above $3,000,000. Surrey's wide range spans from condos, some near the first‑time buyer exemption threshold (up to an $835,000 fair market value), to larger single‑family homes that sit above it. Use the Cash to Close calculator for an exact number.

How far is Surrey from downtown Vancouver?

By transit, the Expo Line SkyTrain runs from Surrey Central to downtown Vancouver, so check TransLink for current trip times from your station. By car, travel time depends heavily on the town centre and traffic.

Is Surrey part of Metro Vancouver?

Yes. Surrey is a member municipality of the Metro Vancouver Regional District and the second‑most populous municipality in the region, after Vancouver.

Does this search include South Surrey?

No. South Surrey has its own dedicated search, since its housing stock and price points differ from much of the rest of the city. This page covers the rest of Surrey, including City Centre, Guildford, Newton, Fleetwood, Cloverdale, and Port Kells.

What school district serves Surrey?

Public schools fall under School District 36 Surrey.
