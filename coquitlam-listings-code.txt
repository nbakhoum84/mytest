          <summary>What's the property transfer tax on a home in Coquitlam, BC?</summary>
          <p>BC's property transfer tax is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% from $2,000,000 to $3,000,000, and a further 2% (5% total) on any residential value above $3,000,000. Coquitlam's price points span a wide range, from Town Centre condos that can qualify for the first&#8209;time buyer exemption (up to an $835,000 fair market value) to Westwood Plateau estates well into the top tax tier. Use the <a href="/cash-to-close">Cash to Close calculator</a> for an exact number.</p>
        </details>

          <summary>How far is Coquitlam from downtown Vancouver?</summary>
