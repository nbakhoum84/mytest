Port Moody, BC

Port Moody Homes for Sale: Condos, Townhomes & Single‑Family Homes

Port Moody is the smallest of the Tri‑Cities and was the original terminus of the Canadian Pacific Railway before the line was extended on to Vancouver. Today it's known as the City of the Arts, with a waterfront centred on Rocky Point Park and a cluster of craft breweries along what locals call Brewers Row. The Millennium Line's Evergreen extension serves the city at Moody Centre and Inlet Centre stations. With hundreds of active listings, prices range widely, from a condo near the station to a home on Heritage Mountain. Whether you're after a condo near Moody Centre, a family home on Heritage Mountain, or a character property near Rocky Point, Port Moody stays smaller by design.

Why Port Moody? The Arts, the Water & Brewers Row

Port Moody stays small by geography, and that's part of what makes it distinct.

🎨 The City of the Arts

Port Moody's official tagline reflects its arts centre, galleries, and community theatre.

Port Moody was the original terminus of the Canadian Pacific Railway before it was extended to Vancouver.

A cluster of craft breweries along Murray Street has made Port Moody a regional destination for beer lovers.

Frequently Asked Questions About Port Moody Real Estate

Is Port Moody a good place to invest in real estate right now?

Port Moody's small size can limit new supply compared to its Tri‑Cities neighbours, and SkyTrain access near Moody Centre and Inlet Centre stations is a draw for many buyers. Talk to Nader for a current‑day read on a specific street.

What's the property transfer tax on a home in Port Moody, BC?

BC's property transfer tax is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% from $2,000,000 to $3,000,000, and a further 2% (5% total) on any residential value above $3,000,000. Port Moody's range spans from condos near Moody Centre station, some near the first‑time buyer exemption threshold (up to an $835,000 fair market value), to larger homes on Heritage Mountain that sit above it. Use the Cash to Close calculator for an exact number.

How far is Port Moody from downtown Vancouver?

By car it's roughly 30 to 60 minutes depending on traffic. By transit, take the Millennium Line SkyTrain from Moody Centre or Inlet Centre station, with a transfer for downtown, so check TransLink for current trip times.

Is Port Moody part of Metro Vancouver?

Yes. Port Moody is a member municipality of the Metro Vancouver Regional District, and the smallest of the three Tri‑Cities municipalities (Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, and Port Moody) by both population and land area.

What school district serves Port Moody?

Public schools fall under School District 43, the same district that covers Coquitlam and Port Coquitlam.
