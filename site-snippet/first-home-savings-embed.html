The rules

First Home Savings Account (FHSA)

  • For first-time buyers 18 or older, living in Canada: you haven't lived in a home you or your partner owned this year or in the last 4 years.
  • Contribute up to $8,000 a year and $40,000 in total. Room starts the year you open the account, and up to $8,000 of unused room carries into the next year.
  • Contributions are tax deductible, and withdrawals for your first home are tax free. Nothing is paid back.
  • The account must be used or closed within 15 years of opening (or by the end of the year you turn 71).

RRSP Home Buyers' Plan (HBP)

  • Withdraw up to $60,000 from your RRSP each, so up to $120,000 for a couple.
  • Money must be in the RRSP at least 90 days before you withdraw it.
  • Pay it back to your RRSP over 15 years. For a first withdrawal up to the end of 2028, repayments start in the fifth year after; otherwise in the second year.
  • You can use the FHSA and the HBP together for the same home.

Minimum down payment

  • 5% of the first $500,000, plus 10% of the part from $500,000 to $1,500,000.
  • Homes at $1,500,000 or more need 20% down.
  • Under 20% down, CMHC insurance is added to the mortgage; the 7% PST on it is paid in cash at closing.

BC first-time buyer transfer tax exemption

  • Up to $8,000 off the property transfer tax on homes up to $835,000, phasing out to $860,000.

Sources: CRA: FHSA, CRA: Home Buyers' Plan, 2026 budget update (HBP grace period to 2028). Checked October 2026.
