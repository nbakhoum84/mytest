## Data note
Lofty and the MLS use the single city name "Langley" for both the City of Langley and the Township of Langley, so the Market snapshot covers both together (the same coverage your page describes). The FAQ explains the difference between the two.

## Claims I changed (they could not be supported)
- Willoughby tied to the SkyTrain extension ("lining Willoughby Town Centre and the future SkyTrain extension"): removed. The line is planned to end at Langley City Centre, not through Willoughby.
- "one of the last areas in Metro Vancouver with large-scale master-planned new construction" and "one of the last stretches of the Fraser Valley corridor still being built out from scratch": softened to "one of the region's major areas".
- SkyTrain timing ("set to reach Langley City Centre by late 2029"): now "currently scheduled for late 2029" (it can change; check TransLink before relying on it).
- "the areas closest to the future Langley City Centre station are already pricing that in": removed (unverified price claim). Nader's Take now poses it as a question for buyers.
- "Fort Langley and Brookswood barely change at all": changed to Fort Langley only (Brookswood is described elsewhere as redeveloping).
- "Langley keeps more open space than most of its neighbours": softened to "plenty of open space".
- "Many Willoughby condos and townhomes fall near the first-time buyer exemption threshold" and "Fort Langley acreages often land in the top tier": softened to "some... can qualify" and "larger acreages can land in the higher tax tiers".
- "expected to shorten" commute time: now "Transit times are expected to improve".

## Check yourself before publishing
- BC property transfer tax figures and first-time buyer thresholds ($835,000 / $860,000, set in 2024).
- SkyTrain extension timing (late 2029) against TransLink's latest announcement.
- Neighbourhood details (for example Murrayville close to the hospital and recreation centre).
- `/langley` (area page) and `/langley-listings` can compete with each other; give them different titles and cross-link them.
