New Westminster, BC

New Westminster Homes for Sale: Condos, Townhomes & Character Homes

New Westminster, known as the Royal City, was the original capital of the Colony of British Columbia before Victoria took over the role in 1868, and it's one of the smallest, most densely built cities in Metro Vancouver today. Both the Expo and Millennium SkyTrain lines serve it, with several stations packed into a compact city. With hundreds of active listings, prices range widely, from a Downtown condo to a character home in Queens Park. Whether you're after a highrise unit along the Quay, a heritage home in Queens Park, or a townhome in Sapperton, New West packs a lot into a small footprint.

Why New Westminster? History, Transit & the Waterfront

New Westminster fits a lot of history and transit into one of the region's smallest cities.

👑 The Royal City

New Westminster was the original capital of the Colony of British Columbia, before Victoria took over the role in 1868.

🚇 Two SkyTrain Lines

The Expo and Millennium lines both serve New Westminster, including the 22nd Street, New Westminster, Columbia, Sapperton, and Braid stations. That's a lot of rapid transit for a small city.

🏥 Royal Columbian Hospital

One of the region's major hospitals, with trauma and cardiac services, is based in Sapperton.

🎓 Douglas College

New Westminster is home to a main campus of Douglas College.

🏞️ The Quay Boardwalk

A riverfront walkway and market along the Fraser River, a short walk from the SkyTrain.

🏘️ Heritage Streets

Queens Park's tree‑lined streets hold some of the city's best‑known character homes.

📚 Schools

Public schools fall under School District 40 New Westminster.

📏 Compact by Design

New Westminster is one of the smallest cities by land area in Metro Vancouver, which is part of why it's so walkable.

Is New Westminster a good place to invest in real estate right now?

New Westminster's small footprint and SkyTrain access tend to support steady demand, particularly for condos near the Quay and Uptown. Talk to Nader for a current‑day read on a specific building or street.

What's the property transfer tax on a home in New Westminster, BC?

BC's property transfer tax is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% from $2,000,000 to $3,000,000, and a further 2% (5% total) on any residential value above $3,000,000. New Westminster's smaller average price points mean some purchases, especially condos, can qualify for the first‑time buyer exemption up to an $835,000 fair market value. Use the Cash to Close calculator for an exact number.

How far is New Westminster from downtown Vancouver?

About 25 to 40 minutes by SkyTrain on the Expo Line, or roughly 25 to 40 minutes by car depending on traffic.

Is New Westminster part of Metro Vancouver?

Yes. New Westminster is a member municipality of the Metro Vancouver Regional District and one of the smallest by land area.

What school district and post-secondary options serve New Westminster?

Public schools fall under School District 40. The city is also home to Douglas College's New Westminster campus.
