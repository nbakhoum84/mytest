Vancouver, BC

Vancouver Homes for Sale – Condos, Townhomes & Character Homes

Vancouver is British Columbia's most populous city and the anchor of the region that shares its name, though the provincial capital is Victoria. The city wraps around Stanley Park and is served by the Expo, Millennium, and Canada Line SkyTrain routes, plus the SeaBus across Burrard Inlet. With hundreds of active listings, prices range widely, from a studio condo to a westside estate. Whether you're after a condo in Yaletown, a character home in Kitsilano, or a townhome on the East Side, Vancouver has almost every kind of property.

Why Vancouver? Parks, Transit & the Waterfront

Vancouver is the region's largest and most varied market, with a neighbourhood for many kinds of buyers.

🌲 Stanley Park

A large urban park at the edge of downtown, with a seawall path around its shoreline.

🚇 Three SkyTrain Lines Plus SeaBus

The Expo, Millennium, and Canada Lines all run through Vancouver, alongside the SeaBus across Burrard Inlet.

🏛️ Not the Capital

Vancouver is BC's most populous city, but Victoria, on Vancouver Island, is the provincial capital.

🎓 UBC & SFU

UBC's Point Grey campus sits beside the city, and SFU has a downtown campus at Harbour Centre.

🛶 Granville Island

A public market and artisan district under the Granville Street Bridge, reachable by small passenger ferry across False Creek.

🏔️ 2010 Winter Olympics Legacy

Vancouver co‑hosted the 2010 Winter Olympics, leaving behind the Olympic Village on the False Creek waterfront.

📚 Schools

Public schools fall under the Vancouver School Board, School District 39.

💰 The Empty Homes Tax

The City of Vancouver charges its own Empty Homes Tax on vacant properties.

Frequently Asked Questions About Vancouver Real Estate

Is Vancouver a good place to invest in real estate right now?

Vancouver's market varies enormously by neighbourhood and property type, and a downtown condo and a westside house behave very differently. Talk to Nader for a current‑day read on a specific neighbourhood or building.

What's the property transfer tax on a home in Vancouver, BC?

BC's property transfer tax is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% from $2,000,000 to $3,000,000, and a further 2% (5% total) on any residential value above $3,000,000. Vancouver's range spans from condos, some near the first‑time buyer exemption threshold (up to an $835,000 fair market value), to houses well above it. The City of Vancouver also charges its own Empty Homes Tax on vacant properties, which is worth understanding before buying an investment property here, so check the City's site for the current rate. Use the Cash to Close calculator for an exact number.

Is Vancouver the capital of British Columbia?

No. Although Vancouver is the province's largest city, Victoria, on Vancouver Island, is the provincial capital.

What SkyTrain lines run through Vancouver?

The Expo Line, Millennium Line, and Canada Line all run through the city. The SeaBus also crosses Burrard Inlet to the North Shore.

What school district serves Vancouver?

Public schools fall under the Vancouver School Board, School District 39.
