## What changed from your existing Delta code

## Claims I changed (they could not be supported)
- "the largest municipality in Metro Vancouver by land area" (twice, in the intro and the FAQ): removed. Surrey and Langley Township are larger.
- "benchmark prices in Ladner and Tsawwassen soften from their peak, even as homes here still sell faster than in most Greater Vancouver communities": removed (unverified and time-sensitive). The FAQ now describes the mix of communities, and the Market snapshot shows real numbers.
- Massey Tunnel replacement "now in early works and procurement": removed (time-sensitive and unverified).
- "more than half the city's population", "built mostly from the 1970s through the 1990s", "dating to the 1860s", "often called the sunniest in Metro Vancouver", "one of the region's mildest microclimates", "former farmland" (Tsawwassen Springs): removed or softened because I could not verify them.
- Car time to downtown is now "roughly 30 to 60 minutes" (it varies a lot between North Delta and Tsawwassen).
- "108,000 residents" is now "about 108,000" (2021 census).
