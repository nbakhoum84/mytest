Delta, BC

Delta Homes for Sale: Coastal Estates, Heritage Character & Family Homes

Delta is home to about 108,000 residents across three distinct communities: the historic fishing village of Ladner, the coastal community of Tsawwassen, gateway to the BC Ferries Tsawwassen terminal, and the family‑oriented suburbs of North Delta. Much of the city remains protected farmland and wetland, including roughly 40 square kilometres of Burns Bog, giving Delta a lower‑density, small‑town feel unusually close to Vancouver. With hundreds of active listings, prices range widely, from a North Delta condo to a Tsawwassen estate. Whether you're looking for a heritage character home in Ladner, a coastal property near Boundary Bay, or a family home in North Delta, Delta has something for every stage.

Lifestyle

Why Delta? Coastal Living, Heritage Character & Room to Breathe

Delta pairs small‑town heritage and coastal beaches with highway and ferry connections across the region.

⛴️ The Tsawwassen Ferry Terminal

BC Ferries' Tsawwassen terminal connects Delta to Vancouver Island and the Southern Gulf Islands.

🌉 Highway 91 & the Alex Fraser Bridge

Delta's northern edge crosses the Fraser River to New Westminster and Surrey via Highway 91 and the Alex Fraser Bridge.

🚗 Highway 99 & the Massey Tunnel

The George Massey Tunnel carries Highway 99 under the Fraser River toward Richmond and Vancouver.

🦢 Reifel Bird Sanctuary

Westham Island's George C. Reifel Migratory Bird Sanctuary draws birdwatchers, especially for the winter snow goose migration.

⛳ Golf‑Course Living

Tsawwassen Springs is a master‑planned golf‑course community at the edge of Tsawwassen.

🌾 Burns Bog & Farmland

Delta protects roughly 40 square kilometres of wetland at Burns Bog alongside extensive Agricultural Land Reserve farmland.

🏖️ Boundary Bay & Centennial Beach

Long, flat beaches make South Delta a favourite for beach walks.

🏫 A School District of Its Own

School District 37 serves Delta on its own, without sharing boundaries with a neighbouring city.

Frequently Asked Questions About Delta Real Estate

Is Delta a good place to invest in real estate right now?

Delta offers a mix of North Delta condos and townhomes with the coastal and heritage homes of Ladner and Tsawwassen, and prices vary widely from one community to the next. Low‑density zoning and protected farmland also limit how much new housing can be built. Talk to Nader for a current‑day read on specific areas.

What's the property transfer tax on a home in Delta, BC?

BC's property transfer tax is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% from $2,000,000 to $3,000,000, and a further 2% (5% total) on any residential value above $3,000,000. Delta's price points span a wide range, from North Delta condos that can qualify for the first‑time buyer exemption (up to an $835,000 fair market value) to Tsawwassen estates well into the top tax tier. Use the Cash to Close calculator for an exact number.

How far is Delta from downtown Vancouver?

Delta has no SkyTrain station of its own. The closest is Scott Road Station on the Expo Line, just across the border in Surrey, reachable by bus from North Delta. By car, it's roughly 30 to 60 minutes to downtown Vancouver via Highway 99 and the George Massey Tunnel, depending on the neighbourhood and traffic.

Is Delta part of Metro Vancouver?

Yes. Delta is a municipality in the Metro Vancouver Regional District, made up of three communities (Ladner, Tsawwassen, and North Delta) and connected to the region by Highway 99, Highway 91's Alex Fraser Bridge, and the Tsawwassen Ferry Terminal.

What school district serves Delta?

Public schools fall under School District 37 (Delta), which serves the city on its own rather than sharing boundaries with a neighbouring municipality. Secondary schools include North Delta Secondary and Seaquam Secondary in North Delta, Delta Secondary in Ladner, and South Delta Secondary in Tsawwassen.
