- "farmers market" at Rocky Point Park: removed (could not verify).
- "Metro Vancouver's Smallest Tri-City" card title: now "The Smallest of the Tri-Cities" (it is not a Metro Vancouver-wide claim).
- "Port Moody's small size limits new supply... which tends to support prices": softened to "can limit new supply" without a price claim.
- "mid-century homes" (Glenayre), "views over Burrard Inlet" (Heritage Mountain), and "growth more contained than Coquitlam's": softened where I could not verify the detail.
- "The Evergreen SkyTrain Line runs through Moody Centre and Inlet Centre stations": now "the Millennium Line's Evergreen extension serves the city at Moody Centre and Inlet Centre stations".
- "original announced terminus of the Canadian Pacific Railway": now "original terminus".

## Check yourself before publishing
- BC property transfer tax figures and first-time buyer thresholds ($835,000 / $860,000, set in 2024).
- Neighbourhood and facility details (for example Eagle Ridge Hospital in College Park, Ioco near Buntzen Lake and Belcarra, Newport Village's plaza).
- Port Moody being the smallest of the Tri-Cities by both population and land area.
- `/port-moody` (area page) and `/port-moody-listings` can compete with each other; give them different titles and cross-link them.
