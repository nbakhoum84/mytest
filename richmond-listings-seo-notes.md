## Claims removed or softened (could not be verified)

- "Fourth-largest city in BC" became "one of the largest cities in Metro Vancouver".
- "Most of it below sea level" became "much of the city is low-lying".
- George Massey Tunnel "eight-lane" and "targeted for 2030" removed. Only "being replaced with a new crossing" remains.
- Richmond Night Market "one of the largest in North America" became "a seasonal night market". It was also moved out of the City Centre/Golden Village description.
- Golden Village "one of the largest concentrations of Asian shopping and dining in North America" became "a dense stretch of Asian restaurants and shopping".
- "Several of Richmond's top-ranked schools" removed from Richmond West.
- Broadmoor "larger lots between Garden City and No. 3 Road" became "established single-family neighbourhood in central Richmond".
- Hamilton "across the Fraser River from Delta" removed (the description did not match its location).
- "Strong demand around Steveston and City Centre" removed from the investing answer.
- "About 25 minutes by Canada Line" and "25 to 35 minutes by car" replaced with a pointer to TransLink, since times vary by station and traffic.
